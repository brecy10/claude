# A. TGV process & industrialization (subagent summary, snippets-based; WebFetch unavailable)
## Via formation
- LIDE (LPKF): min dia ~10 um; AR typ 1:10, up to 1:50; taper 0.1-30 deg (SJTU tool 0.7-8 deg); hourglass (2-side) or V (1-side); >5000 vias/s (Vitrion); sidewall roughness "under investigation" (IMAPS 2025). LPKF 2025 revenue EUR115.3M; Korea downstream yield not yet sufficient (FY2025 call).
- fs laser + etch: KOH selectivity up to 955x vs HF 66x (Ross & MacLachlan, Opt Express 2018), max rates ~320 (HF) / 363 (KOH) um/h; KOH/water/IPA up to 820 um/h, selectivity ~3000, AR 1:1000 channels (Butkute 2022); Bessel single-pulse KOH 606 um/h, 2103:1 (Micromachines 2024 15:1313); Kim et al. Micromachines 2023 14:1766 Bessel two-pulse, 8 M KOH 110C; EPFL arXiv 2107.11220 optimum dose 10x lower.
- Corning laser damage & etch: 25-100 um (Shorey & Lu, IMAPS).
- fs burst drilling AR>1:80, 350 ms/via (SPIE PW 2025 13351-2); KrF >1000 vias/s; engineered-pulse 10 um zero taper, >10000 vias/s (SPIE 12872, 1287205, 2024); CO2 HAZ from pulse tail.
- Photosensitive FOTURAN: exposed etch ~20x faster, wall slope ~20:1(10:1 typical); FOTURAN II 0.5-1 mm, up to 200x200 mm; CETC-58 2023 AR 8:1, min dia 25.68 um (集成技术 2023).
- ECDM: Zou 2023 J Manuf Proc 99, 300 um glass, ~344 um entrance; AR 11 (Kamaraj 2013).
- Sandblasting ~50 um limit; DRIE 0.5-1 um/min.
## Metallization
- Cu-glass adhesion weak; Ti/Cu PVD seed; ZnO-based 5-10 nm sol-gel adhesion layer: peel >8 N/cm (IMAPS, attribution unverified); ~1 kN/m on aluminoborosilicate.
- Xiamen Univ (Yu Daquan) review electroless TGV metallization 电子与封装 25(7):070107 (2025); Ji, Ling, Wu, Yu, Yu, Li, J Electrochem 28(6):2104461 (2022).
- Binghamton superconformal butterfly fill: AR6 3h, AR10 12.5h (JES 2015 162 D457); with MTT 1h/4h (JES 2017 164 D289); 50 um holes in 300-500 um Corning glass.
- Full fill blind vias survived 500 cycles -40/125 (GT PRC/Atotech, 3D InCites 2014); G scales with via diameter; Parylene-F interlayer reduces fracture prob. up to 73% (MRS Spring 2026 abstract).
